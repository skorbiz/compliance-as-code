Compliance for the Nimbus platform covers the legal, regulatory, and standards-based expectations that affect the product, operations, and customers. This section is the starting point for understanding those expectations, how we assess their relevance, and where we maintain supporting evidence.

:::warning Current active work
The **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** is currently the only active compliance initiative in this proof-of-concept. The CRA work focuses on the cybersecurity obligations that apply to Nimbus as an industrial IoT platform connecting on-site equipment to the cloud.

Other legislation and standards are tracked here as context and for future assessment. Their presence in this section does not mean that a separate compliance project is currently active.
:::

## Cyber Resilience Act

The **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** is an EU regulation that sets cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements across their whole lifecycle. The active compliance work is organised around the following areas:

| Obligation             | Page                                         | Purpose                                                         |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Risk assessment**    | [Risk Assessment](./02-product-security/risk-assessment.mdx)     | Identify, evaluate, and mitigate cybersecurity risks.           |
| **Manual**             | [Manual](./02-product-security/manual.mdx)                       | Provide user-facing security information and instructions.      |
| **Incident reporting** | [Incident Handling](./02-product-security/incident-handling.mdx) | Report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents. |
| **SBOM**               | [SBOM](./02-product-security/sbom.mdx)                           | Maintain a Software Bill of Materials for all components.       |
