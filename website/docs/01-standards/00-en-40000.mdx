:::warning
The **EN 40000** numbering is provisional. The harmonised European standards for the CRA are still under development, so final numbering, scope, and structure may change.
:::

The EN 40000 series is intended to provide harmonised European standards that support conformity with the Cyber Resilience Act once the relevant standards are published and cited in the Official Journal.
