This folder records my working notes on the standards and frameworks I use to interpret the CRA and related compliance requirements in this proof of concept.
The idea is to have some easily referenceable notes, so they should point to a specific clause or section; they do not reproduce the standard.
The point is to keep enough context to understand *why* a clause matters, instead of merely citing standards without context.

:::note
These are condensed personal study notes, not a substitute for the standards themselves. Standards such as the EN 40000 series and IEC 62443 are copyrighted works of their respective standards bodies (CEN/CENELEC, IEC, national bodies like Dansk Standard). The notes here only summarise selected clauses in my own words for orientation — always obtain and read the official text before relying on it.
:::
