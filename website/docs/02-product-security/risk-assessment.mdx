## Risk assessment methodology

Nimbus follows the risk-assessment workflow of **[IEC 62443-3-2:2020](../01-standards/03-iec-62443-3-2-2020.mdx)**.
The workflow starts by defining the [Product Context](../03-product-context/product-context.mdx) — the system under consideration (SUC),
its security perimeter, zones, conduits, and access points — and then performs the initial and detailed risk
assessment over that context.

See the [Standards](../01-standards/README.mdx) folder for the underlying standards.

### Risk scoring matrix

Risk score is defined as **impact × likelihood**, generated from the scale defined in
`model/risk_model.yaml` so the matrix, bands, and categories below always match the data.

### Risk treatment

For every risk we record an explicit treatment decision, following
**[IEC 62443-3-2:2020](../01-standards/03-iec-62443-3-2-2020.mdx)** ZCR 5.7 and ZCR 5.11 (compare the risk against
tolerable risk, then decide). Our **tolerable-risk line** is the 🟡 medium band above: risks at or below it are
tolerable, while serious and above call for a deliberate treatment decision.

- **Undecided** — a risk with no recorded decision yet.
- **Accept** — the risk is tolerated as-is; no further action is required.
- **Transfer** — the risk is shifted to a third party (e.g. insurance or a vendor).
- **Mitigate** — the risk is being reduced through additional controls.
