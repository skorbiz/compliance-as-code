## Missing disk encryption


### At a glance

- **Edge / Cloud:** Edge
- **Conduit / exposure area:** Disk
- **Phase:** All
- **Category (IEC 62443-3-3 FR):** Data Confidentiality
- **IEC 62443-3-3 requirement inspiration:** —
- **Risk treatment:** Mitigate (1 planned action)

### Risk scenario

1. **Vulnerability** — Missing full-disk encryption on edge devices
2. **Event / threat** — A person with physical access (opportunistic thief or non-malicious employee, low skill, minimal resources) obtains an edge device through theft, loss, or decommissioning and reads the unencrypted disk directly
3. **Consequence** — Customer and machine data stored on the edge device is disclosed

### Risk score

:::tip[🟢 Initial risk: 2 — Low]
**Tolerable (2)** impact × **Improbable (1)** likelihood
:::

#### Additional controls

- **Enable disk encryption** — Enable full-disk encryption on all new devices (Owner: `core-team`, due `2027-01-01`)

:::note
Disk encryption is not currently enabled on edge devices.
:::
